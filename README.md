The Moon finally answers back, in one line. After four billion years of
wishes, howls and 2 a.m. promises from everyone staring up at it, the Moon
replies — funny, a little fed up, and keeping receipts.

`main.py` (Python 3.13 standard library only) writes that single line as
UTF-8 text to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it. Deterministic: the same
line every run.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## The line

> Four billion years of wishes, howls, and 2 a.m. promises -- and my answer is: I heard every one of them, I kept the receipts, and no, I am not your therapist.
